Please consider our manuscript, “State Preservation Is Not Output Equivalence:
Conditional Error and Pipeline-Cost Analysis of Change-Gated Video Depth,” for
publication as an article.

The manuscript examines a fixed change-gated selective state-space depth model.
It distinguishes exact state copying from dense-history approximation, derives
conditional defect/cache/refresh bounds and an additive cost criterion, and tests
the scope of those statements using controlled implementation comparisons. A frozen
evaluation on four reserved TUM sequences and reproducible development diagnostics
accompany the analysis. We explicitly report the limited end-to-end sparse speed
gain and avoid claims of global accuracy certification or general superiority.
